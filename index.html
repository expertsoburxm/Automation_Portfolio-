How the system works

Every workflow follows the same backbone: capture the lead, notify the right person, and keep following up until the lead either responds or is handed off — with no gaps for a lead to fall through.

CAPTURE

Form submitted

A custom GoHighLevel form (quote request, booking, popup, or ad lead) triggers the workflow the instant it's submitted.

ROUTE

Notify & assign

The opportunity is moved to the right pipeline stage, a task is created, and the lead is assigned to the correct team member.

NURTURE

Email + SMS sequence

Confirmation, thank-you, and follow-up messages go out automatically across both channels on a set schedule.

ADAPT

Branch on behaviour

The workflow checks whether the lead opened an email or booked a call, then branches into a different follow-up path.
